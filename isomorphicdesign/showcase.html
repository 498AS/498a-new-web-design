04 — Layout & narrative indents

Editorial indents do the work of grids.

• RESEARCH

Towards a foundation model of biology.

Our work centres on a simple idea: that biology, like language, has structure — and that structure can be modelled. Across the proteome, across binding events, across the dynamics of a cell, patterns repeat in ways that learning systems can capture.

The goal of the lab is not to automate a small piece of the discovery pipeline. It is to compress the timeline from idea to candidate by orders of magnitude — and to extend the surface of disease that medicine can reach.
